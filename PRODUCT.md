# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students of Alexandria University (mostly Faculty of Engineering) who are ACM chapter members, committee/club board members, high board, and admins. On their own profile they check their role, committee, and season points, and keep their contact details (phone, LinkedIn, batch, department) up to date. Admins and HR also open other members' profiles to review and manage points.

## Product Purpose
The official site of the ACM Alexandria Student Chapter: showcases events, clubs, programs, committees, and radio, handles registrations, and gives members and admins the tools to run the chapter. Success is members finding what they need quickly and trusting the data shown about them.

## Brand Personality
Technical, crisp, student-run. A confident engineering club: clean and precise, a little playful, never flashy.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS card grids: identical rounded cards with an icon and a tiny uppercase label.
- Glassy gradients: blurs, glows, gradient text, neon accents.
- Busy dashboards: hero metrics, stat tiles, chart clutter.

## Design Principles
- Information first: show the member's data plainly, with clear hierarchy instead of decoration.
- Respect the existing system: brand blue #4B98C8, slate neutrals, Roboto, light and dark themes.
- Earned emphasis: color and weight only where they carry meaning (role, points, actions).
- Student-made, not template-made: specific structure and copy over generic scaffolding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes, keyboard-accessible controls, and reduced-motion alternatives for any animation.
